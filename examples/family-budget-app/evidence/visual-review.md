# Visual Review

## Direction

“Financial kitchen table”: editorial serif typography, ledger-like mono labels, warm paper, deep household green, and envelope-inspired budget cards. Family goals remain beside monthly spending instead of being buried in analytics.

## Passed

- Clear desktop hierarchy from breathing room to actions, envelopes, goals, and ledger.
- No visible overlap, clipping, or horizontal overflow at 1440px.
- At 390px, identity and members remain visible, actions become full-width targets, and envelopes collapse to one column.

## Residual checks

- Dialogs need automated focus trapping before production.
- Google Fonts should be self-hosted for privacy and offline use.
- Full keyboard and screen-reader audits remain required.
